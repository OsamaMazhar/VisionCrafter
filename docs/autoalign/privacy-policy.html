Privacy Policy

Auto Align by orbitaar

Your Privacy Matters

At Orbitaar, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Auto Align handles your information.

1. Data Collection and Usage

Auto Align does not collect, store, or share any personal data, images, or usage analytics. All image processing is performed locally on your device. No photos, edits, or personal information are transmitted to Orbitaar or any third parties.

2. Third-Party Services

Auto Align does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app does not integrate with external APIs that collect or process user data.

3. Security

Since no data is collected or transmitted, your privacy and security are inherently protected. We recommend keeping your device secure and updated to further protect your information.

4. Children's Privacy

Auto Align is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page. Your continued use of Auto Align after changes are made constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or your privacy when using Auto Align, please contact us: info(dot)orbitaar(at)gmail(dot)com
