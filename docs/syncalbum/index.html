Powerful Features

Everything you need for seamless photo sharing.

1

Instant Real-Time Sharing

Share photos the moment you capture them. No waiting, no batching. Friends see your photos instantly as you upload them.

2

Dedicated Albums

Create isolated albums for family vacations, milestones, parties, and events. Each album is completely separate from your main photo library.

3

Collaborative Pools

For parties and events, create a shared pool where everyone can contribute. All participants see every photo in one place, instantly.

4

Category Organization

Group your albums into custom categories like "Family Vacations," "Children's Milestones," or "Business Trips" for quick filtering.

5

GPS & Date Tagging

Every photo is automatically tagged with precise GPS coordinates and capture dates. Know exactly where and when each moment happened.

6

AI-Powered Story Generation

Automatically creates beautiful, cinematic stories from your album photos using On-device AI. The app intelligently selects your best moments, groups them by location and date, and generates compelling narratives—all processed locally on your device for maximum privacy. No data ever leaves your device. Perfect for journaling your children's growth or reliving family vacations.

7

Cross-Device Sync

Your albums sync automatically across iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Start capturing on one device and continue seamlessly on another.

8

No Subscriptions

Flexible pay-as-you-go model—purchase points once and use them anytime. 1 point = 1 photo upload. Points never expire.

9

Photo Journaling

Write your personal journal alongside your photos. Add your thoughts, memories, and descriptions to create lasting narratives. Perfect for documenting your children's milestones with personal notes or capturing family vacations with your own perspective. Save your journals with editable chapters and access them anytime.

More Great Features

Additional capabilities that make SyncAlbum stand out.

Smart Face Detection

Advanced On-device AI detects faces and intelligently positions them in story playback and album covers. All processing happens locally—no data ever leaves your device.

Ken Burns Effects

Watch your stories come alive with smooth, cinematic pan-and-zoom animations.

Custom Music

Choose from curated royalty-free music or import your own tracks for personalized soundtracks.

Privacy-First Design

No backend, no data collection. Your photos stay in your iCloud. Only you and approved friends can see them.

Built-in Camera

Carefully designed camera with intuitive gesture controls for zoom and exposure. Photos save directly to your album—no import needed. Supports up to 48MP on compatible devices.

4K Video Export

Export stories as high-quality videos in 4K or 1080p. Choose between cinematic 30fps or smooth 60fps.

Memory Efficient

Store photos at display quality to save device space. Full-resolution versions remain in iCloud.

Guest Pool Mode

Album owners can purchase photo upload credits that all participants can use. Perfect for family trips.

Saved Memories

Save your favorite stories and photo journals with editable chapters. Access saved memories anytime.

Revoke Access Anytime

Revoke access to any friend. Their albums will be automatically deleted from their devices.
