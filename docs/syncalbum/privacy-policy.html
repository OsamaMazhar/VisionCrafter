Privacy Policy

SyncAlbum by Orbitaar Solutions · Effective 13 November 2026 · applies to SyncAlbum 5.0 and later

In Short

  • Your photos, videos, comments, journal entries and voice notes are never sent to us. They live in your own iCloud, or your own Google Drive if you choose.
  • No SyncAlbum account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. We do not sell personal data.
  • To make sharing work, a few sharing records (display name, coded identifiers, join requests, activity notices) are kept in a shared iCloud database. We delete them on request.

Who We Are

Orbitaar Solutions, KvK 98502050 (address in the business register at kvk.nl), is the controller for the data described here.

What We Process

Album content. Photos, videos, comments, journal entries and voice notes (audio and transcripts) are stored in the album's iCloud space or your Google Drive, where album members can see them. Drive files are encrypted on your device before upload. Private journal notes stay in your own private iCloud. Apple and Google store this under your own agreement with them; we never receive it.

Sharing records. Sharing needs a few records in an iCloud database that Apple hosts for SyncAlbum, which any signed-in iCloud user can technically read:

  • your display name and a coded Apple Account identifier (not your email or phone number);
  • join requests and answers (identifiers, status, dates). Since 5.0 they carry no album name and their details are encrypted so only the owner can read them; an approved request can still show the member's display name. Requests from older versions can show the album name and the requester's display name;
  • notices that an album was deleted or someone was removed;
  • activity notices ("new photo"): coded identifiers of who acted and who is told, album and item identifiers and the activity type, never names or content;
  • encrypted invites and, while an album is being made private, a short-lived pointer to its new link that only approved members can open.

Android notifications. Our small service on Cloudflare sees coded album identifiers and the sender's IP address, keeps the IP in memory for about a minute to prevent abuse, and stores nothing. It asks Google Firebase to wake the other phones with a content-free message, using a device token.

Google ML Kit (Android) runs on your phone for features such as photo labels, face framing and QR scanning. Your photos are not sent. ML Kit sends Google usage statistics: device model, system and app version, an installation identifier, timings and error codes.

Place names. If you allow location access, photo coordinates are sent to Apple, or Google on Android, to look up the place name.

On your device only: face-aware framing (it finds where faces are, never who they are), stories, private albums and the diagnostic log, which reaches us only if you send it.

Support email: your address and what you send. Purchases go through Apple and Google; we get no payment details.

Website. orbitaar.com and its invite pages run on GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses for security. No cookies or trackers. The secret part of an invite link (after "#") is never sent to the website.

Who Else Is Involved

  • Apple: iCloud, the shared database, notifications, place names, App Store.
  • Google: Drive and sign-in (if chosen), Firebase notifications, place names on Android, Google Play, ML Kit.
  • Cloudflare (notification service), GitHub (website) and our email provider (support).

Acting for us, they may use the data only for that purpose; acting for themselves, their own privacy policies apply. We disclose data to others only if the law requires it.

Data is processed in the United States and elsewhere. Transfers from the EEA, UK and Switzerland rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (with its UK and Swiss extensions) or standard contractual clauses. Album members can be anywhere, so sharing records can be read in any country, which is necessary to provide shared albums (GDPR Art. 49(1)(b)).

Sharing Is Your Choice

An album is shared only when you share its link or QR code, and only people the owner approves get in. The owner can remove anyone. Members see your display name and everything you add, and can save copies while they have access; we cannot recall them. Add content about others only if you are allowed to.

Albums shared before SyncAlbum 5.0 use an older type of link. To make sure only people you approve can open one, use Album menu › Make Private.

Your Rights

In the app you can delete photos and albums (an album you own is deleted for everyone), leave albums, change your display name and turn off location access. Deleting the app removes only its data on the device, not your iCloud data or sharing records.

By email you can ask for access, a copy (portability), correction, deletion or restriction. You can object at any time to processing based on legitimate interest, and withdraw consent at any time, for example by turning off location access for SyncAlbum.

To delete your sharing records, email us with the subject "Delete my data"; we do so within 30 days. This can stop shared albums from working for you. As there is no account, we may ask for details to find your records and confirm the request is yours. We reply within one month.

You can complain to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl) or the authority where you live.

Security

Data travels encrypted. Drive files, join-request details and album keys are encrypted, and new shared albums are private. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts you at high risk, we will tell you as the law requires, in the app and on our website.

Children

SyncAlbum is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. Under 16, or your country's age, use it only with a parent's permission. A parent can email us to delete a child's sharing records.

Specific Regions

  • United States, including California: we collect identifiers (display name, coded account IDs, IP address, email if you write) and album activity, from you and other members, only to run shared albums and give support, and no sensitive personal information. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising and have not in the past 12 months. You can ask to know, correct or delete your data, also through an authorised agent, without being treated differently.
  • UK: same rights under the UK GDPR; complaints to the ICO. Switzerland: complaints to the FDPIC.
  • Brazil: LGPD Art. 18 rights; complaints to the ANPD. India: send grievances to the contact below; we answer within one month.
  • Canada, Australia, Japan: your data is held outside your country, mainly in the US.

Changes

We may update this policy. The new version is posted here with a new date; important changes are noted in the app's release notes.
