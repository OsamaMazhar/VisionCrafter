Privacy Policy

SyncAlbum by Orbitaar Solutions · Effective 15 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data SyncAlbum uses on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android, why, and what your rights are. Extra information for some countries is at the end. It describes SyncAlbum 5.1. Where an older version works differently in a way that matters for your privacy, this policy says so. Please keep SyncAlbum up to date; we may ask you to update before the app can upload again.

The Short Version

  • Your photos, videos and comments are not sent to us. They are stored in iCloud (and, if you choose, in your own Google Drive).
  • There is no SyncAlbum account. We do not use advertising or tracking, we do not run analytics, and we do not sell personal data. One Google component on Android sends usage statistics to Google if you allow it.
  • To make sharing work, a small set of sharing records (your display name, account identifiers, join requests and activity notices) is kept in an iCloud database that we manage. Other iCloud users can read these records. You can delete them in the app, or email us if you can't.

We are responsible for the sharing records, the Android notification service and support email described below. We are not required to appoint a data protection officer.

The content of your albums is stored in iCloud or Google Drive under Apple's and Google's agreements with the account holder. We have no access to it. You decide what goes in your albums and who you share them with, and you are responsible for that.

Where Your Albums Live

Albums, photos, videos, captions, comments, reactions, favourites and journal entries are stored in Apple's iCloud under an Apple Account, on every platform. SyncAlbum for Android also keeps albums in iCloud, so it needs an Apple Account too. Apple protects this data as described in its own iCloud security documentation.

When you share an album, the people you approve can see it and add to it. What they add is stored in your iCloud. Albums you join are stored in their owner's iCloud.

Google Drive (optional). You can keep an album's full-resolution files in your own Google Drive instead of iCloud. You then sign in with Google, and SyncAlbum asks for access only to the files it creates. The app encrypts these files on your device before upload. Your Google account's name and email are shown in the app and are not sent to us.

Private and hidden albums. Private albums are kept on your device and are not synced. Hidden albums can be locked with Face ID, Touch ID, or your Android fingerprint or face unlock. The operating system does the check; SyncAlbum does not receive your biometric data.

Sharing and Joining

You share an album by sharing its link or QR code. People who open the link in SyncAlbum ask to join, and the app lets them in after the owner approves. Your display name and the photos, comments and reactions you add are visible to everyone in that album.

Albums shared before SyncAlbum 5.0 use an older iCloud link that iCloud itself does not lock. Someone who has that link and uses it outside SyncAlbum could see and add to the album until the owner removes them. The owner can lock the link with Make Private in the album menu.

People in an album can save copies of what is in it. Those copies stay with them after their access ends or the album is deleted, and we cannot control or delete them.

Add photos, names and comments about other people only if you are allowed to.

Sharing Records We Process

To let people find each other and join albums, the app writes records to an iCloud database that Apple hosts for SyncAlbum. We manage this database and can see these records. Other iCloud users, not only the people in your albums, can read them. Some records about you are written by other members' apps, for example when an owner answers your request or removes you.

  • Display name and account identifier. Your display name, a pseudonymous identifier and a date. The identifier is a code, not your email or phone number, but it stays the same and is linked to your display name. This record is published when you share an album, join one or ask to join one.
  • Join requests and answers: identifiers of the person asking, the album owner and the album, the status (such as pending, approved, declined, blocked or left), dates, and technical fields the app uses to check the request. The request's details are encrypted for the album owner. The album and requester name fields hold fixed placeholders.
  • Membership notices: that an album was deleted or someone's access was removed, with identifiers and dates.
  • Make Private: while an album is being made private, a record holds the album's new iCloud link in readable form. That link opens only for people the owner approved. The record is removed when the move finishes.
  • Activity notices that tell members something changed, such as a new photo or comment: identifiers of the person who did it, the person to tell, the album and the items concerned, the type of activity and a count. They hold no photo, comment text or names.
  • Invites, stored as encrypted and signed data.

These records do not contain your photos, videos or comments. Versions before 5.0 wrote more in readable form, such as the album's name in a join request.

You need a display name and an Apple Account signed in to iCloud to share or join albums. Without them, sharing does not work.

Other Data We Process

Android notifications. A notification service run by us and hosted by Cloudflare tells Android phones when an album changes. It receives codes that stand for albums, and the sending phone's network (IP) address. It uses the address for a short time to limit abuse and is not built to keep it. The service then asks Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging to wake the other phones. That message carries no photos, names or text. Firebase uses a notification token for the app on each phone.

On-device features on Android. SyncAlbum for Android uses Google's ML Kit for features such as recognising what is in a photo, finding faces for framing, reading text and scanning QR codes. ML Kit works on your device. According to Google, ML Kit sends Google usage and performance statistics, such as device model, system version, app version, an installation identifier, timings and error codes. From SyncAlbum 5.1 the app asks before it first uses ML Kit, and you can change your choice in Settings › Privacy (Google ML Kit Features). If you say no, these features stay off.

Place names. If you allow location access, the coordinates of a photo are sent to Apple (iPhone, iPad, Mac) or Google (Android) to look up the place name.

Faces. Face-aware framing finds where faces are in a picture. SyncAlbum does not recognise or identify people and does not create face prints or other biometric identifiers.

Stories and journal. Video stories, journal entries and voice-note transcripts are made on your device and are not sent to us.

Diagnostics. SyncAlbum keeps a diagnostic log on your device. We receive it only if you choose to send it. The app has no crash reporting of its own. Apple and Google may give us crash and usage statistics that do not identify you, if you agreed to share them with developers.

Support. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send.

Purchases. Purchases are handled by Apple or Google Play. We do not receive your payment details.

Website. Our website and its invite pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses under its own privacy policy. We do not use cookies, analytics or trackers on the website. Browsers do not send the part of an invite link after "#" to the web server.

Service notices. From SyncAlbum 5.1 the app regularly checks our website for a short notice from us, so we can reach users when needed, for example about a security issue. The app adds no identifiers to the request, but GitHub sees your IP address, as with any website visit. A notice can require an update before an older version uploads again.

Sharing records. From SyncAlbum 5.1 the app removes sharing records you created when they are no longer needed:

  • activity notices: after about 30 days;
  • join requests still waiting for an answer: after about 90 days;
  • declined, expired or left join requests and their answers: about 30 days after the last change;
  • notices that an album was deleted or access was removed: about a week after the other person's app has confirmed it, and otherwise after about 12 months;
  • your display name, approved requests, blocks and invites: kept, because the albums you share or belong to need them. They are not removed automatically. Delete your sharing data to remove them.

The cleanup runs when SyncAlbum 5.1 or later is opened, so these times are not exact. If you stop using SyncAlbum or keep an older version, your records stay until you delete them or ask us to. Removing records you still need can stop a shared album from working for you.

We do not use your data for profiling or automated decisions.

Permissions and Data on Your Device

SyncAlbum asks for access to your photos and videos, camera, location, microphone and speech recognition, biometric unlock and notifications to provide the related features: importing photos, the built-in camera, place names, voice notes, locking hidden albums and alerts about album activity. You can change a permission in your device's Settings. Features that need a permission you did not grant will not work.

The app stores your albums, settings, keys and notification token on your device because its features need them.

Your Rights

Within the limits of the law, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct data that is wrong (you can change your display name in the app);
  • delete your data;
  • restrict the processing of your data;
  • give you your data in a common, machine-readable format.

What we can delete for you. If you can't use the app, for example because you deleted it or use an older version, email contact(at)orbitaar(dot)com with the subject "Delete my data". We can delete your display name, your join requests and answers, and the notices about you. Settings › Privacy shows your SyncAlbum ID; include it so we can find your records. If you use the Email us button there, the app also saves a short code for about 30 days so we can check that the email comes from you. We delete the records we can identify as yours within the time the law sets.

What only you can delete. Only you, or the album's owner, can delete:

  • photos, videos, comments, likes and journal entries, because they are in the owner's iCloud or Google Drive;
  • your membership of an album: leave it in the app, or ask the owner to remove you;
  • your invites, because they are stored encrypted and we cannot tell whose they are: use Stop Sharing All, or send us the invite link;
  • private albums and anything on your devices;
  • copies other people saved.

You can object at any time to processing that we base on our legitimate interests (see the table above). Where processing is based on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time: location in your device's Settings, ML Kit in SyncAlbum's Settings › Privacy (Google ML Kit Features). Withdrawing consent does not affect what happened before.

Most of your data is in your own iCloud or Google Drive, where you can view, export and delete it yourself. To delete sharing records in the app, see "Deleting Your Data". For anything else, email us. Requests are normally free. We answer within the time the law sets, which in the EU is normally one month. Because there is no SyncAlbum account, we may ask for details that let us find your records and check that the request comes from you. If we cannot identify your records, we may not be able to act on the request.

You can complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl), or to the authority where you live or work.

Deleting Your Data

Album content. Delete photos or albums in the app, or leave albums you joined. Deleting an album you own removes it for the people in it. You can also manage SyncAlbum's data in your Apple Account settings and in Google Drive. Signing out or deleting the app does not delete what is in iCloud. Private albums exist only on the device and can be lost with the app.

Sharing records. Deleting the app does not remove your sharing records. From SyncAlbum 5.1 you can remove them in Settings › Privacy › Delete Sharing Data. You can choose to:

  • delete your sharing data: you leave the albums you joined, and the app removes what you added to them and your requests and notices. Albums you share stay shared, so your display name, your answers to join requests and your invites stay;
  • stop sharing all and delete everything: the same, and the albums you own stop being shared and stay on your devices. Your display name and invites are removed too.

When you leave an album, a note that you left stays for about 30 days so the owner's app can remove you. If something cannot be reached or removed, the app tells you, and you can ask the album's owner to remove what is left.

Security

We use measures that fit the data we handle, such as encrypted connections and owner approval for shared albums. No system is fully secure, and we cannot guarantee security. Keep your devices and your Apple and Google accounts safe. If a data breach is likely to put you at high risk, we will tell you as the law requires.

Children

SyncAlbum is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you are under 16, or under the age your country sets for agreeing to online services, use SyncAlbum only with a parent's or guardian's permission. Parents who believe their child has used SyncAlbum without permission can email us, and we will remove the child's sharing records that we can identify.
