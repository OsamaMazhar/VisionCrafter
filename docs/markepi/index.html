Content Credentials (C2PA)

Anyone can add a watermark. Almost no consumer app can cryptographically seal who you say made a photo, how it was made, and whether it has been altered since — on-device, with no cloud. Markepi does.

Prove your photo is real.
Prove it's yours.

In the age of AI-generated imagery, Markepi seals a signed, tamper-evident C2PA manifest into your exports. It's signed with a key created on your device — held in the Secure Enclave, where it cannot be exported, on hardware that supports it. No upload, no account, no cloud signing service. Any C2PA-aware tool can check the seal, anytime.

  • On-device signing, Secure Enclave-backed where available
  • Tamper-evident — any edit breaks the signature
  • Preserves prior provenance as an ingredient
  • Evidence-gated claims — you can't sign a lie
  • Reads & verifies incoming Content Credentials
  • Plain-language Export Receipt after every sign

Text Watermarks

Watermark not just stills but full videos (with audio) and Live Photos (with motion). Very few watermark apps do all three.

What Markepi does
