Take a closer look.

Know what it's for.

Great at

  • Buildings, interiors, real estate, cityscapes and travel
  • Posters, billboards, whiteboards and artwork shot at an angle
  • Receipts, letters, cards and slides, scanned and filed
  • Finding a document by any word on it, or where you scanned it
  • Keeping HDR, EXIF, GPS and the original, always revertible

Not made for

  • Recreating parts of a building cut out of the frame
  • Extreme perspective beyond a certain limit
  • Scenes with no straight edges, where Manual Fix does the job instead
  • Ask Documents on devices without Apple Intelligence, or in Arabic, where the button isn't shown
  • Syncing documents between your devices
