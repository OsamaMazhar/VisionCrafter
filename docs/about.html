About orbitaar.

A small studio making apps with computer vision and on-device AI.

orbitaar builds apps that use computer vision and on-device AI to do one job well. The aim is to blend that technology with design simple enough for anyone to use.

It started with AutoAlign, which straightens leaning buildings and now scans and answers questions about your documents. SyncAlbum followed for shared family albums, and Markepi for watermarks, frames and Content Credentials. See the apps ›
