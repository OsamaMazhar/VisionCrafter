About VisionCrafter

Who is VisionCrafter?

VisionCrafter is the creative identity of a passionate indie developer focused on building innovative photography and creative tools. With a background in urban photography and a love for technology, VisionCrafter aims to make photo editing easier and more accessible for everyone.

This site showcases apps developed by VisionCrafter, starting with Auto Align and expanding to more tools in the future.
