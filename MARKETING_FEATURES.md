# SyncAlbum - Marketing Features Document

## The Problem

Sharing photos with friends and family has always been tedious. You go on a trip, capture hundreds of memories, and then what? You have to wait until later, carefully select photos, and send them in batches via AirDrop. Apple's Shared Albums help, but the process is still clunky—you can't share instantly in the moment, and managing multiple shared albums becomes overwhelming.

You want separate, dedicated albums for different occasions—family vacations, children's milestones, parties, and events. You want to journal every single day of your children's growth and write your best memories. But your main photo library becomes a mess of mixed content, and you don't want shared photos cluttering your personal collection.

For events and parties, you need a shared pool where everyone can take photos and everyone sees them in the same place, instantly. No waiting, no batching, no "I'll send them later."

**SyncAlbum solves these problems.**

---

## What SyncAlbum Does

SyncAlbum is a privacy-focused photo sharing app that creates separate, dedicated albums for every occasion. Share instantly with friends and family, contribute in real-time, and keep your shared memories completely separate from your personal photo library.

---

## Top 10 Main Features (Hero Features)

### 1. Instant Real-Time Photo Sharing
Share photos the moment you capture them. No waiting, no batching, no "I'll send them later." Friends see your photos instantly as you upload them. Perfect for parties, events, and trips where everyone wants to see memories as they happen.

### 2. Separate Dedicated Albums for Every Occasion
Create isolated albums for family vacations, children's milestones, weekend getaways, parties, and events. Each album is completely separate from your main photo library—no clutter, no mixing of personal and shared content. Perfect for maintaining dedicated albums for your children's growth journey.

### 3. Collaborative Photo Pools
For parties and events, create a shared pool where everyone can contribute. All participants see every photo in one place, instantly. No more "who has which photos?" or piecing together memories from multiple sources.

### 4. Category-Based Organization
Group your albums into custom categories like "Family Vacations," "Children's Milestones," "Weekend Getaways," or "Business Trips." Filter by category to quickly find specific memories without scrolling through everything.

### 5. GPS Location Tagging & Date Assignment
Every photo is automatically tagged with precise GPS coordinates and capture dates. Fix your memories by location and time—know exactly where and when each moment happened during your trip or event.

### 6. AI-Powered Story Generation
Automatically creates beautiful, cinematic stories from your album photos. The app intelligently selects your best moments, groups them by location and date, and generates compelling narratives. Perfect for journaling your children's growth or reliving family vacations.

### 7. 48MP High-Resolution Photo Capture
Capture stunning detail with professional-grade 48MP photos. Every memory is preserved in crystal-clear quality that looks amazing on any display. Your children's milestones deserve the best quality.

### 8. Triple Camera System (Ultra-Wide, Wide, Telephoto)
Switch seamlessly between three professional lenses: 0.5x Ultra-Wide for group shots and landscapes, 1x Wide for everyday moments, and 3x+ Telephoto for distant subjects. Get the perfect perspective for every occasion. Intuitive gesture controls for smooth continuous zoom and sliders for manual exposure settings.

### 9. Cross-Device Synchronization
Your albums sync automatically across iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Start capturing on one device and continue seamlessly on another. Your family can contribute from their own devices, and everyone sees the same album in real-time.

### 10. Points-Based Photo Upload System (No Subscriptions)
Flexible pay-as-you-go model—purchase points once and use them anytime. 1 point = 1 photo upload. Points never expire, giving you complete control over your spending. No recurring subscriptions, no hidden fees.

## 9 Additional Features (Supporting Features)

### 11. Smart Face Detection & Photo Positioning
Advanced AI detects faces in your photos and intelligently positions them in story playback and album covers. Ensures people are always perfectly centered and visible across all your devices—great for family photos and children's portraits.

### 12. Animated Story Playback with Ken Burns Effects
Watch your stories come alive with smooth, cinematic Ken Burns pan-and-zoom animations. Each photo transitions beautifully with professional motion effects that create an engaging viewing experience for reliving memories.

### 13. Custom Music for Stories
Choose from a curated library of royalty-free music or import your own tracks. Trim music to the perfect starting point and create personalized soundtracks for your stories—perfect for setting the mood of family vacations.

### 14. Editable Story Narratives & Photo Journaling
Customize AI-generated stories with your own text. Edit chapter titles, add personal descriptions, and create unique narratives that tell your story your way. Perfect for photo journaling—capture moments, add your thoughts, and create lasting memories. Ideal for journaling your children's milestones with personal notes or documenting family vacations with your own perspective.

### 15. Privacy-First Design with Full Control
Face detection data only stores anonymous bounding boxes—no facial features or biometric data are ever used. Your privacy is protected while still enabling smart photo positioning. Shared albums are separate from your main photo library.

**No Backend, No Data Collection** - Your photos stay in your iCloud. We don't have a backend server or collect your data. Only you and friends you explicitly approve can see your shared photos.

**iCloud Storage Only** - People on iPhone just need enough iCloud storage for their albums to stay in them. No additional subscriptions to Google, OneDrive, or Dropbox. Your privacy is our main focus.

**Prevents Duplication** - Photos taken from the app only save in the SyncAlbum app and no duplication happens. Your photos stay organized without cluttering your main library.

**Revoke Access Anytime** - Whenever you want, you can revoke access to any friend. Their albums will be automatically deleted from their devices because you've revoked their access. You're always in control.

**Approval-Based Invitations** - Share a link, but only people you approve can join. No strangers accessing your albums. You decide who gets in.

**Disable Further Invitations** - Want to stop new people from joining? Disable the share link from your own device. The link will no longer be shareable, giving you complete control.


### 17. Professional Video Export (4K/1080p)
Export your stories as high-quality videos in 4K resolution or share-friendly 1080p. Choose between cinematic 30fps or smooth 60fps frame rates. Supports both portrait and landscape orientations. Perfect for sharing family vacation videos on social media.

### 18. Memory Efficient Storage
Option to store photos at display quality to save device space. Full-resolution versions remain in iCloud and download automatically when you save to Photos or export. Great for devices with limited storage.

### 19. Guest Pool (Owner-Subsidized Mode)
Album owners can purchase photo upload credits that all participants can use. Perfect for family trips where one person covers the cost for everyone's contributions—no asking friends to buy points.

### 20. Saved Memories & Photo Journals
Save your favorite stories and photo journals with editable chapters. Access saved memories anytime, even from albums you no longer actively use. Perfect for preserving your children's growth stories as permanent keepsakes. Capture moments, add your thoughts, and create lasting photo journals.
