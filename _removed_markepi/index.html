Content Credentials (C2PA)

Anyone can add a watermark. Almost no consumer app can cryptographically prove who made a photo, how it was made, and that it hasn't been altered since — on-device, with no cloud. Markepi does.

Prove your photo is real.
Prove it's yours.

In the age of AI-generated imagery, Markepi seals a signed, tamper-evident C2PA manifest into your exports. It's signed with a Secure Enclave key that physically cannot leave your device — no upload, no account, no cloud signing service. Any C2PA-aware tool can verify it, anytime.

  • On-device, Secure Enclave-backed signing
  • Tamper-evident — any edit breaks the signature
  • Preserves prior provenance as an ingredient
  • Evidence-gated claims — you can't sign a lie

  • Plain-language Export Receipt after every sign

Powerful Features

Everything you need to mark, protect, and prove your work.

1

Text Watermarks

Add your name, brand, handle, or copyright line in a choice of typefaces, with full control over color, opacity, size, and position. Stack multiple text layers.

2

Logo / Image Watermarks

Drop in a PNG or image logo from Photos or Files. Add several logos, resize them, set transparency, and place them anywhere on the image.

3

Hand-Drawn Signatures

Sign your work with your finger or Apple Pencil on a real drawing canvas. Adjust ink color and stroke thickness for a personal touch a font can't replicate.

4

White Frame + Smart Captions

Add a clean gallery-style white border with an optional caption that auto-fills from your photo's EXIF data — camera, lens, aperture, shutter, ISO, date, and more. No typing required.

5

Retro Date Stamps

Burn a warm, glowing film-camera "databack" date into the corner of your photo, in six authentic formats — using the photo's real capture date.

6

Photos, Videos & Live Photos

Watermark not just stills but full videos (with audio) and Live Photos (with motion). Very few watermark apps do all three.

7

HDR & Metadata Preservation

Your HDR gain map, GPS, capture date, camera info, and color profile all survive the watermark — where most apps flatten and strip them.

8

Batch Processing (up to 20)

Apply one watermark configuration to a whole set at once — mixing photos, videos, and Live Photos — with per-photo overrides when a single image needs something different.

9

⭐ Content Credentials (C2PA) — the flagship

Cryptographically sign your photos on-device with a tamper-evident C2PA manifest — the open standard behind Adobe, the BBC, Sony, Nikon, and Leica. Embed IPTC rights metadata, declare honestly how the image was made, preserve any existing provenance as an ingredient, and get a verifiable Export Receipt — all with a Secure Enclave key that never leaves your device.
